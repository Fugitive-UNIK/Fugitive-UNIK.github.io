About Me / 关于我
------
I'm a graduate student from [Advanced Studies in Statistics and Data Science](https://asds.cuhk.edu.hk/) in the [Department of Statistics and Data Science](https://www.sta.cuhk.edu.hk/) at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html). I love using statistics to tackle challenging data analysis problems, and I believe that statistics is a philosophy about uncertainty, which requires peeling back the veil of uncertainty step by step to explore the truth behind the data. My research interest focus on Machine Learning, Time Series and Fractional-order Grey System.  

我是来自[香港中文大学（CUHK）](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html)的[统计与数据科学系](https://www.sta.cuhk.edu.hk/)的[统计与数据科学研习](https://asds.cuhk.edu.hk/)的研究生。我热爱运用统计方法解决复杂的数据分析问题，并认为统计是一种关于不确定性的哲学，它需要逐步揭开不确定性之面纱，以探索数据背后的真相。我的研究兴趣主要集中在机器学习、时间序列以及分数阶灰色系统方面。

You can find my CV here: [English Version](../assets/XinyuHuang_CUHK_ASDS.pdf) or [Chinese Version](../assets/黄欣雨_香港中文大学_统计与数据科学.pdf).
